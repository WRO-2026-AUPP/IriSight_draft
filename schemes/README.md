# International electronics and wiring

This directory is the entry point for the rebuilt International robot's wiring,
power architecture, component map, pin assignments and safety documentation.

The final International diagram still needs to be produced from the rebuilt
robot. Confirmed current information includes the custom ESP32 board, one
JGB37-520 12 V 100 RPM drive motor, MG996R steering servo, Hokuyo UST-30LX,
rear VL53L0X, BNO055 and 1 MP USB camera.

The former National diagrams, component photos and editable Draw.io source are
preserved under [`legacy_national/`](legacy_national/). They must not be used as
the final wiring instructions without comparison against the rebuilt robot.
