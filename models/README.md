# International mechanical models

This directory is the entry point for the rebuilt International robot's CAD,
print settings, mechanical drawings and design iterations.

The updated CAD files have not yet been added. When available, the current
editable source, printable exports and dimensioned renders will be placed here
and documented by component.

The former National Competition CAD is preserved under
[`legacy_national/`](legacy_national/) as development-history evidence. It
includes the previous RealSense camera mount and must not be treated as the
final International design.
