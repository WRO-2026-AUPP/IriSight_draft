🎥 Video Demonstration
====

This section links to historical demonstrations of the autonomous vehicle.
Only YouTube links are committed; raw and edited local video files are kept
outside the repository.

### Open Challenge

The linked demonstration shows three completed laps and an autonomous stop,
with no manual intervention after starting.

### Obstacle Challenge

The linked demonstration shows parking exit, full-lap operation and parking
entry without manual intervention. The recording does not include all three
complete laps, so it is treated as proof of running rather than a final
full-run result.
